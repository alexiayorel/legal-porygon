actual — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Nature of the application

actual is a private, non-commercial application built and used by a private individual for their own personal use. It is not offered to any third party, is not commercialised, and has no users other than its owner.

Its sole function is to retrieve the transactions of its owner's own bank accounts and record them in a self-hosted instance of the open source budgeting software Actual Budget.

Data processed

  • Account holder identity, account identifiers (IBAN), balances and transactions, as provided by the bank through the API of Enable Banking Oy, an Account Information Service Provider (AISP) authorised and supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA).
  • No banking authentication credentials are ever seen, handled or stored by the application: strong customer authentication takes place entirely at the bank.

Legal basis and consent

Processing is based on the explicit consent of the account holder, who is also the sole user of the application. Consent is given during strong customer authentication at the bank and may be withdrawn at any time, either with the bank or by deleting the application in the Enable Banking Control Panel.

Storage and hosting

Data is stored exclusively on a private server located at the owner's home in Belgium. It passes through no third-party service other than Enable Banking, which acts as the technical intermediary to the banks.

Sharing

None. Data is never sold, rented, transmitted, or analysed for advertising or statistical purposes. No analytics tooling is used.

Deletion

Data can be deleted at any time by the owner, who has direct access to the server hosting it.
