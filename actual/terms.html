Last updated: 27 July 2026

Purpose

actual is a private application built by a private individual for their own use. It synchronises the transactions of its owner's bank accounts into a self-hosted instance of Actual Budget, for personal budgeting purposes.

Users

The application has exactly one user: its owner. It is not made available to the public, is not distributed, and no third-party account can be connected to it.

Non-commercial use

The application is not associated with any commercial or professional activity. It does not access any business account. No service is sold, resold or provided to any third party on the basis of the data processed.

Access to banking data

Account access is performed through the API of Enable Banking Oy, an authorised Account Information Service Provider (AISP), under the European PSD2 directive. Authorisation is granted by the account holder through their bank's strong customer authentication, for a limited period, and may be revoked at any time.

No warranty

The application is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It is not intended to produce accounting or tax records, and the data it displays constitutes neither an official bank statement nor financial advice.
